Professional background

Our roots are in U.S. international taxation and transfer pricing. Since the early 2000s our work has centered on Chinese tax — inbound investment and outbound expansion by Chinese enterprises.

Our experience includes leadership of international tax for China and Asia-Pacific at a Big Four firm — advising Fortune 500 companies and smaller enterprises on complex transactions, integrating tax with customs, foreign exchange, and overlapping legal frameworks.

As in-house tax leadership for multinationals (China-focused and global roles), covering expansion into Europe, North America, and Latin America — designing protocols, automation, controversy strategy, and teams; advising on new initiatives; mitigating risk; defending audits.

In law-firm settings, leading the most complex projects, where technical accuracy, strategic judgment, and negotiation are equally critical.

Scope of experience

  • Multinationals operating across jurisdictions
  • Senior management navigating regulatory and tax uncertainty
  • Corporate tax functions needing strategy + execution

Industries include manufacturing, technology, consumer goods, hospitality, engineering projects, and digital models. The distinguishing feature: both external advisory and in-house leadership — technically rigorous, operationally grounded.

Nature of work

Focus on complex, non-routine matters where strategy, sequencing, and execution matter as much as interpretation:

  • Tax audits and dispute resolution
  • Transfer pricing planning, documentation, negotiations
  • Cross-border M&A and post-transaction restructuring
  • Supply-chain and operational restructuring
  • Advance pricing arrangements
  • Treaty interpretation and withholding tax

Many matters involve prolonged engagement with authorities, resolved through detailed analysis and sustained negotiation — often without adverse adjustments.

Writing and thought leadership

Active writer and speaker on tax topics; regular professional commentary and technical analysis; invited to academic and professional forums.
