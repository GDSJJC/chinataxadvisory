China's 2025 Encouraged Foreign Investment Catalogue Widens the Gateway to Tax and Customs Incentives

Effective February 1, 2026, the expanded Catalogue adds a net 205 entries — with them, eligibility for reinvestment credit, import duty relief, and reduced regional EIT.

What is new

NDRC/MOFCOM Order 37 (Dec 2025) replaces the 2022 version: 1,679 entries (+205 net, 303 revised), national + regional (central/west/northeast + Hainan). New: nucleic-acid drugs, medical/smart detection, robotics components, gas turbines, high-end shipping, virtual power plants, plus consumer services (pet care, sports tourism, homestays).

Why encouraged status matters

Reinvestment credit (MOF/STA/MOFCOM No. 2 [2025]): 10% credit on profits reinvested into national-encouraged enterprises (2025–2028, 5-year hold; treaty rate caps, e.g. 5% HK — can bring withholding near nil).

Customs: self-use equipment + accompanying tech/parts/spares duty-exempt within total investment (duty only, VAT since 2009; exclusion lists apply).

EIT: 15% vs 25% for encouraged enterprises in west/Hainan. Plus priority land at ≥70% minimum price for intensive industrial projects.

Transition and timing

Approved/filed ≥Feb 1, 2026 → 2025 Catalogue. Earlier approvals in 2025 Catalogue may claim new; 2022-only keeps eligibility to Feb 1, 2027. Customs sequence: Confirmation Letter → duty-reduction review → before import (no retroactive relief). Watch 6-month confirmation validity, supervision periods, amendment on scope change.

Action points

  • Re-map each project/capital increase to 2025 codes (AC national, HB regional); identify added/revised/removed.
  • Equipment: letter + review before shipment.
  • Reinvestment: confirm national-catalogue target, 5-year hold, 2025–28 window, treaty cap.
  • West/Hainan new projects: weigh 15% EIT in location choice.
