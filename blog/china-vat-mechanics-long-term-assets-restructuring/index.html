China Tax Advisory Mar 28, 2026 VAT Law

China Sets the Mechanics for Long-Term-Asset Input VAT, Asset-Restructuring Relief, Mixed Sales, and the Time of Supply

Announcements No. 13 and No. 15 of 2026 implement the VAT Implementing Regulations — expanding non-taxable restructuring while tightening conditions, fixing mixed-sale rates, and adding timing for prepaid services.

Input VAT on long-term assets (Announcement 15)

Fixed assets, intangibles, immovable property used only for standard taxable projects: fully creditable; only for non-creditable: none. Mixed-use ≤RMB 5M original value: fully credited. Above RMB 5M: credited on acquisition then adjusted yearly over 20y (property/land), 10y (aircraft/trains/ships), 5y (others) — clawback from depreciation × sales mix in January following year (first: Jan 2027 for 2026 assets). Net-value formulas for change-of-use/disposal; upgrades added to original value. Build the asset-by-asset VAT ledger in 2026.

Announcement 13 simplifies vehicle VAT (invoice direct) and rail/air e-tickets (amount shown, no formula).

Non-taxable restructuring

Relief now expressly covers goods, financial products, intangibles, immovable property as a permanent regime — but requires: package move with claims/debts/employees; independently operable business; reasonable commercial purpose; transferee general taxpayer if transferor is. Confirmed not non-deductible (input stays creditable); unused-credit carryover narrowed to mergers; no filing procedure stated — document and seek certainty pre-close.

Mixed sales: four fixed patterns

Software+install/maintenance/training → software rate; board houses/machinery/steel structures+install → goods rate; charging/battery-swap (electricity+bundled) → 13%; vehicle rental+info services → rental rate. Goods-with-install split/simplified option removed — cost can rise. Genuinely separable, separately contracted/priced components remain two transactions. Mark main business in contracts.

Time of supply

Long-cycle (>12mo) goods: on payment/contract date regardless of delivery. Immovable property: earlier of title vs. delivery. Financial-institution accrued interest: 90-day rule carried over (institution definition drops securities funds). Material change: prepaid installment services taxed in full on earlier of first delivery or contract date — hits telecom, training, leasing, property management, F&B. Reassess cash-tax and refund handling.

Recommendations

  • Build VAT ledger + data logic in 2026 for Jan 2027 adjustment.
  • Confirm restructuring tests with authority given no filing path.
  • Map bundles to new rates; update contracts/invoicing; reset prepaid timing.
